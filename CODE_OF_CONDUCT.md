# Code of Conduct

This is a small project. The rules fit on a page.

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person on the other side is doing their best with less context than you have.

## What that means here

- **Criticise the app, not the person.** "This screen made me lose ten minutes and here's why" is
  useful and welcome, however blunt. Contempt aimed at a person is not.
- **No harassment**, and none of the usual: insults, slurs, sexual attention, doxxing, or
  publishing anyone's private information. This includes other people's data that shows up in
  screenshots — yours or a stranger's.
- **Nobody is owed a feature.** This is one person's work, given away for free. Ideas are read; not
  all of them get built, and "no" is an answer.
- **Don't be a support burden on other users.** Anyone helping in Discussions is a volunteer.

## Scope

Issues, discussions, pull requests, release comments, and any private channel opened from them
(security reports, email about this project).

## Enforcement

Reports go to **bymaksymdev@gmail.com** and stay private. The maintainer may edit, hide, lock or
delete contributions, and may block accounts, with or without warning depending on severity. There
is no committee and no appeal process — again, one person.

If the report is about the maintainer's own behaviour, GitHub's
[Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines)
and abuse reporting apply, and that is the right channel.

Written in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), kept
short on purpose.
